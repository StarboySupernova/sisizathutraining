LAB 01: Connectivity Outage

Scenario: You are a Junior Systems Administrator. At 09:00 AM, the primary business application crashed. Users cannot connect to the server. You must authenticate, run the diagnostics, fix the config file, and log the incident using corporate best practices.

Phase 1 Governance & Tracking

Log in to projects.sisizathutraining.com.
Move the task "Investigate Hub Connectivity" to In Progress.

Phase 2 Diagnostics & Repair

In the Mobile Terminal (Right), click "RUN DIAGNOSTICS".
Enter your Sisizathu Admin Username to authenticate.
Observe the CRITICAL FAULT. The system will crash and enter Lockdown.
Switch to the Code Editor tab on the device.
Fix the config.json file to point to lab.sisizathutraining.com instead of localhost. Save & Run to restore the system.

Phase 3 SLA & Knowledge Transfer

Open a ticket at support.sisizathutraining.com. Attach a screenshot of your success.
Write a resolution guide at knowledgebase.sisizathutraining.com to document the fix.
